# Experience Guide — Café Time Period Timelapse

Per-era detail notes for the five café interiors (1945 → 2025). Each era shares
one architectural shell (a ~7 m × 5 m × 3 m room with a front window wall, an
entrance door, and a long counter run along the left wall) but transforms every
audit category so the room reads as "one café evolving" across eighty years.

---

## 1945 — Post-war Café

**Furniture & decor.** Heavy dark-oak café tables, a round table, and Thonet
bentwood chairs with woven cane backs. Warm terracotta/mint accents. Lace and
damask curtains at the front window. A worn checkerboard linoleum floor.

**Coffee & brewing equipment.** A tall manual lever espresso machine with
brass pull handles and a wall-mounted grinder with a mechanical crank.

**Menu & prices.** A 1945 board: coffee 7¢, pie 15¢, milk 5¢ — ration-era
prices.

**Music source.** A walnut valve wireless set on the counter (the era's radio),
with a glowing tuning dial.

**Posters & ads.** "Buy War Bonds / Save & Serve", a sugar-ration notice, an
"ICE COLD COCA-COLA" sign, a "Victory in Europe" newspaper headline, and a
period café photograph.

**Tableware.** Thick white porcelain cups with gold rims, a glass water
carafe, heavy oak serving pieces.

**Signage & lighting.** Brass pendants, wall sconces, and warm low-wattage
tungsten bulbs; a "Café Entrance" decal.

**Counter tech.** A hand-crank mechanical cash register with a pop-out drawer.

**Patrons.** Suits, victory-roll hair, fedoras and hats — guests reading the
paper with a coffee.

---

## 1965 — Mid-Century Espresso Bar

**Furniture & decor.** Teak tables with chrome splayed legs, molded plastic
bucket / vinyl-and-chrome chairs, a warm terracotta-and-mint area rug, string
art and macramé.

**Coffee & brewing equipment.** A chrome-and-bakelite espresso machine with a
glass dome and steam wands (Faema-style), chrome sugar dispensers, a glass cake
stand.

**Menu & prices.** A plastic letterboard: coffee 15¢, espresso 20¢, milkshakes
25¢, sandwiches 30–35¢.

**Music source.** A chrome-and-glass jukebox with a glowing arch trim and a
visible stack of colourful 45s (5¢ / 3 plays).

**Counter signage & ads.** Bold 60s travel posters and a hand-painted
instant-coffee ad, a starburst clock, sunburst wall art, string art.

**Tableware.** Pastel melamine mugs, chrome-rimmed glasses, milkshake glasses
with straws, ashtrays, checkered napkin dispensers.

**Signage & lighting.** A neon "OPEN" sign, a sputnik pendant, warm café lamps,
a letterboard menu.

**Counter tech.** An electric round-key cash register with a receipt printer.

**Patrons.** Slim suits and skinny ties, shift dresses, beehive hair, leather
jackets, teens at the jukebox, a transistor radio.

---

## 1985 — Neon Café

**Furniture & decor.** Black laminate tables with tubular chrome legs, black
vinyl cafe chairs, rubber plants, a mirrored wall panel, a Memphis squiggle
mural, an arcade corner.

**Coffee & brewing equipment.** A boxy chrome espresso machine with a digital
keypad facade, a drip-brew tower with glass decanters on warmers, a glass
cookie jar.

**Menu & prices.** A backlit lightbox menu: fresh coffee 55¢, espresso 75¢,
cappuccino 85¢, croissant 65¢.

**Music source.** A silver twin-cassette boombox with VU meters and an antenna
on a shelf.

**Posters & ads.** "Live Tonight / The Neon Kids" and "Rock 85" gig flyers, a
"Grand Opening" poster, an Employee of the Month photo, a Coffee Talk
community board.

**Tableware.** Ceramic mugs, sugar dispensers, teabag caddies, a Rubik's cube
on one table, number stands.

**Signage & lighting.** Pink and teal neon strips, track spotlights, a CRT TV
playing static.

**Counter tech.** An early electronic POS with a green segment display and a
dot-matrix customer screen.

**Patrons.** Members-Only jackets, perms and feathered hair, shoulder pads,
legwarmers, sneakers, a Walkman, a pager, a Polaroid.

---

## 2005 — Early Wi-Fi Café

**Furniture & decor.** Dark wood tables with wrought-iron legs, a plush
banquette, warm neutral floor.

**Coffee & brewing equipment.** A stainless E61-style semi-automatic espresso
machine with a PID temperature panel, a pastry case with labeled trays.

**Menu & prices.** The Daily Grind: coffee $1.50–$2.50 (tall/grande/venti),
lattes $2.25, frappuccino $2.50, fair-trade + organic.

**Music source.** A white click-wheel iPod in a speaker dock with CDs for
sale.

**Counter, signage & ads.** A free Wi-Fi sign, a community board, indie-night
gig posters, an @ decal.

**Tableware.** White ceramic cups, paper to-go cups with cardboard sleeves,
travel mugs, glass water bottles, raw sugar packets.

**Signage & lighting.** Exposed-bulb pendants and a halogen track.

**Counter tech.** A touchscreen POS with a card-swipe reader, a beige CRT
iMac-style order station.

**Patrons.** Bootcut jeans, chunky white sneakers, flip phones, digital
cameras, early iBook laptops, chunky white earbuds.

---

## 2025 — Contemporary Café

**Furniture & decor.** A long communal oak slab table, small round
marble-effect tables, designer stools, bentwood chairs, a living-wall shelving
unit with ceramics, monstera and hanging pothos.

**Coffee & brewing equipment.** A sleek multi-boiler espresso machine with a
touchscreen and pressure/flow readouts, a pour-over station (gooseneck kettle,
V60s, digital scale), a batch brewer into insulated carafes.

**Menu & prices.** A minimal sans-serif menu board: flat white $4.50–$5.50,
cold brew $5, oat/almond milk +50¢, QR-code table-ordering stickers.

**Music source.** A smartphone on a wireless charging stand paired with a
small smart speaker — streaming from the phone to the speaker.

**Posters & ads.** Local-roaster posters, abstract art prints, a community
notice shelf, a chalk A-board.

**Tableware.** Double-wall glass / speckled stoneware cups, keep-cups,
ceramic pour-over servers, matte black cutlery.

**Signage & lighting.** Globe + linear LED pendants, a brand wall decal.

**Counter tech.** An iPad-as-register POS with a contactless tap-to-pay reader,
tablet order screens, and oat/almond milk cartons.

**Patrons.** Oversized fits, cropped wide jeans, chunky sneakers, a laptop +
headphones, a smartwatch, a phone on a mini-gimbal filming a latte, a tote bag,
earbuds in a charging case.

## Music note

All era music beds are **synthesized period-styled audio** generated live with
WebAudio (big-band swing for 1945, jukebox / record-drop for 1965, cassette /
tape-hiss for 1985, click-wheel for 2005, smart-chime / notification for
2025). They are not licensed period recordings. Each era's bed crossfades in
sync with the ~2.5 s timelapse transition.
